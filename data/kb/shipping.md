# Shipping Policy - Lumen Market

## Delivery times and shipping costs
Standard delivery arrives within 5 days of the order date and costs €4.95. Standard delivery is free for orders over €50. Express delivery arrives within 2 days of the order date and costs €9.95. Every order shows an estimated delivery date in the customer's account.

## Countries we ship to
Lumen Market ships to the Netherlands, Belgium, Germany and Luxembourg. We do not currently ship outside these four countries.

## Tracking an order
As soon as an order leaves our warehouse, its status changes to "shipped" and it receives a tracking number. Orders with status "processing" have not left the warehouse yet and have no tracking number.

## Late or missing parcels
If a parcel has not arrived by its estimated delivery date, please allow up to 5 extra days, as carriers are sometimes delayed. If a parcel is more than 5 days past its estimated delivery date, a member of our support team will open an investigation with the carrier and arrange a replacement or refund. Late-parcel investigations are always handled by a human support team member.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can only be changed while the order status is "processing". Address changes are handled by a human support team member. Once an order has shipped, the address can no longer be changed.
