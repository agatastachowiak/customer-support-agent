# Frequently Asked Questions - Lumen Market

## Payment methods
Lumen Market accepts iDEAL, credit card (Visa, Mastercard), PayPal and Klarna (pay later).

## Customer service opening hours
Our human support team is available Monday to Friday, from 9:00 to 17:00 (CET). The online assistant is available at any time and passes complex requests on to the human team.

## Payment problems and double charges
If a customer was charged twice or sees an unexpected charge, our billing team investigates. Payment problems are always handled by a human support team member.

## Personal data and account deletion
Requests to view, change or delete personal data, or to close an account, are handled by our privacy team in line with the GDPR. These requests are always passed on to a human team member.

## Complaints
Customers who want to file a formal complaint can do so through the support team. Complaints are always handled by a human support team member.
