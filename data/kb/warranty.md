# Warranty Policy - Lumen Market

## Warranty periods
Every product has a warranty that starts on the delivery date. Most electronics and kitchen appliances (headphones, speakers, kettles, espresso machines, smartwatches, laptops) have a 24-month warranty. Backpacks have a 12-month warranty. Yoga mats have a 6-month warranty.

## What the warranty covers
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and faults that appear during normal use. It does not cover accidental damage (such as drops or water damage), normal wear and tear, or damage caused by misuse.

## How to make a warranty claim
To make a warranty claim, the customer provides the order number and a short description of the fault. A support team member assesses the claim and arranges a repair or replacement. Warranty claims are free for the customer, including shipping.
