# Returns and Refunds Policy - Lumen Market

## Return window
Customers can return any item within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund. The 30 days are counted from the date the order was delivered, not the date it was ordered. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. After 30 days from delivery, items can no longer be returned, but faulty items may still be covered by the warranty.

## How to return an item
To return an item, the customer logs in to their account, selects the order and chooses "Return item". Returns from the Netherlands and Belgium are free. Returns from Germany and Luxembourg cost €6.95.

## Refund timing
Refunds are issued within 14 days after the returned item arrives at our warehouse. The refund goes to the original payment method. Banks may take another 3 to 5 business days to show the refund on the customer's statement.

## Cancelling an order
An order can only be cancelled while its status is "processing", meaning it has not left the warehouse yet. Customers can cancel through their account, or ask a support team member to do it. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled; the customer can return it after delivery instead.

## Damaged or faulty items on arrival
Items that arrive damaged or faulty must be reported within 14 days of delivery, ideally with photos. For items under €500, the customer can return the item for a free replacement or full refund using the normal return process. Damage claims for items worth €500 or more are always handled by a human support team member, who will arrange collection and a replacement.
